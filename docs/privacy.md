# Spacekeeper privacy policy

*Last updated: 19 October 2026*

Spacekeeper is made to keep your photos yours. In short: **the developer
collects nothing.** Your photos are read on your iPhone and go only to the
cloud account or the computer you choose.

## What the app reads, and where it stays

- **Your photo library.** With your permission, Spacekeeper reads your photos
  and videos on the iPhone to show their sizes and to copy the ones you choose.
  This happens on the device. Nothing about your library is sent to the
  developer or to anyone else.
- **What it remembers.** A list of item sizes and the items on their way to
  your cloud are kept in the app's own storage on your iPhone, so it does not
  have to measure everything again. Deleting the app deletes them.
- **Sign-ins.** When you connect Yandex Disk or Google Drive, the access token
  the service issues is kept in the iOS keychain on your iPhone. It is sent only
  to that service. You can sign out in the app at any time, and withdraw access
  in your Yandex or Google account settings.

## Your clouds

Files you move or copy go directly from your iPhone to your own Yandex Disk or
Google Drive account. The developer has no access to them. The privacy policy
of Yandex or Google applies to what their services store.

On Google Drive, Spacekeeper asks only for access to the files it creates
itself (the `drive.file` permission). It cannot see anything else in your
Drive. Spacekeeper's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs
adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Backup to a computer

The backup to a computer works only over the cable, only while the Backup
screen is open, and only for a computer that knows the code shown on it.
Nothing is reachable over a network.

## What the developer does not do

- No accounts with the developer, no analytics, no crash reporting services,
  no advertising and no tracking.
- No selling or sharing of any data, because none is collected.

If this ever changes, for example if the app starts showing ads, this policy
will say so beforehand and the App Store listing will be updated.

## Children

Spacekeeper is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
