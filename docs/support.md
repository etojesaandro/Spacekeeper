# Spacekeeper support

**Report a problem or ask a question:**
[open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/etojesaandro/Spacekeeper/issues).
Please tell which iPhone and iOS version you have, and what you expected to
happen.

## Common questions

**Is anything deleted without asking me?**
No. An original is removed only after its copy in the cloud has been checked
against it, and iOS asks you to confirm every deletion. Deleted items stay in
*Recently Deleted* for 30 days.

**The space did not come back after a move.**
iOS frees the space when *Recently Deleted* is emptied: Photos › Albums ›
Recently Deleted.

**How do I back up to my computer?**
Open the Backup tab, connect the iPhone with a cable, start `spacekeeper-backup`
on the computer and type the code the phone shows. Windows needs *Apple
Devices* from the Microsoft Store (or iTunes); when asked, tap *Trust* on the
iPhone. Downloads are on the [GitHub page](https://github.com/etojesaandro/Spacekeeper).

**Can I bring photos back?**
Yes: in the Cloud tab open a photo and tap Restore. Live Photos come back as
Live Photos; edits made in Photos come back as the original.

**How do I disconnect a cloud?**
Tap the account button at the top of any tab and choose Sign out. You can also
withdraw access in your Yandex or Google account settings.
