# Spacekeeper

**Free up space on your iPhone without losing a single photo.**

Spacekeeper lists every photo and video with its size, biggest first, and
moves the ones you choose to a place that keeps them: Yandex Disk, Google
Drive, or your own computer over the cable. An original leaves the iPhone only
after its copy has been checked byte for byte, and iOS still asks before
anything is deleted.

- **See what takes the room** — a grid like Photos, with each item's size; by
  year and month, with what each period weighs.
- **Move, Copy or Delete only** — uploads go on in the background, into
  year and month folders, with the dates the photos were taken.
- **Bring them back** — browse your cloud and restore originals, Live Photos
  included, to Photos.
- **Back up to a computer** — Windows, macOS or Linux, over the cable, no
  internet needed.
- **In your language** — English, Russian, Ukrainian, German, Spanish, French,
  Italian, Portuguese, Chinese and Japanese.
